ATEK303

An acid firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: the ATEK303 voice from VCV Rack with its own pattern generator. Press PLAY and a line plays.

Play it in your browser Cheat sheet Download (.fwsc)
The browser version runs the firmware's own code, compiled to WebAssembly (fm1-base's emulator). Computer keys and the mouse play it.
