Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

An acid machine: the ATEK303 voice from VCV Rack with its own pattern generator. Press PLAY and a line plays.

14 oscillators, 64 steps with a piano-roll editor, drive, chorus, a synced delay and a plate reverb, 12 factory presets in 32 slots, MIDI in, out and clock.

Play it in your browser Install on your FM-1 Back up presets Cheat sheet Download .fwsc

Version 1.2 · Chrome or Edge to install, over USB

The browser version runs the firmware's own code, compiled to WebAssembly (fm1-base's emulator). Computer keys and the mouse play it.
